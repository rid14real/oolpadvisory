## Before going live — recommended

1. **Self-host the images.** They currently load from the Pexels CDN. Download each
   photo and place it in `assets/`, then update the `src` paths. This removes any
   dependency on an external service.
2. **Replace with real firm photography** when available — photos of the actual team,
   office and client work will always outperform stock.
3. **Leadership headshots.** The About page deliberately uses initial monograms, not
   stock portraits — using photos of strangers to represent named directors would be
   misleading. Swap in real headshots of Aderemi and Oluwafunmilola when ready.
